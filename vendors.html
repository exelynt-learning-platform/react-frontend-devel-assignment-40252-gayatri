Vendor Guidelines

We encourage all our vendors to maintain product quality and provide a friendly shopping experience.

  1. Sell fresh and quality products.
  2. Maintain cleanliness at the stall.
  3. Display product prices clearly.
  4. Use environmentally friendly packaging.
  5. Treat every customer respectfully.
