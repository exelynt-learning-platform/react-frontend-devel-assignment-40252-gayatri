Visit Green Valley Farmers Market

Spend your weekend exploring fresh local products, meeting farmers, and enjoying a friendly community shopping experience.

Market Opening Hours

  • Saturday: 8:00 AM - 2:00 PM
  • Sunday: 8:00 AM - 2:00 PM
  • Monday to Friday: Closed

Tips for Visitors

Follow these simple tips to make your farmers market visit enjoyable.

  1. Arrive early for the freshest products.
  2. Bring reusable shopping bags.
  3. Carry cash for convenient shopping.
  4. Explore different vendor stalls.
  5. Ask vendors about seasonal products.

Facilities Available

  • Parking area for visitors
  • Drinking water facility
  • Seating area
  • Family-friendly shopping space
  • Reusable shopping bags available at selected stalls
