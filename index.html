Welcome to Happy Paws

Happy Paws Animal Shelter is a caring community dedicated to rescuing abandoned animals and finding them safe, comfortable and loving homes.

We believe every pet deserves kindness, proper care and a family that loves them.

Meet Our Animals

How You Can Help

There are several ways you can support our shelter:

  • Adopt an animal and give it a loving home.
  • Volunteer your time to care for animals.
  • Donate food, blankets and other supplies.
  • Share our shelter information with friends and family.

Our Adoption Process

  1. Visit our shelter or browse available pets online.
  2. Choose an animal that suits your family.
  3. Complete an adoption application.
  4. Meet the animal and speak with our team.
  5. Welcome your new family member home.
