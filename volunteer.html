Help Us Make a Difference

Volunteers are an important part of Happy Paws Animal Shelter. You can help animals feel safe, healthy and loved.

Volunteer Opportunities

  • Feed and provide water to animals.
  • Walk and play with dogs.
  • Help clean animal living areas.
  • Assist with adoption events.
  • Help with fundraising and awareness campaigns.

Volunteer Application Process

  1. Complete the volunteer application form.
  2. Our team will review your application.
  3. Attend a short introductory meeting.
  4. Choose your preferred volunteer activity.
  5. Start helping animals at our shelter.
